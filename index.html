01 About

As a Research Analyst at Forte Capital Group, I contribute to market research and financial analysis to support investment decisions. In prior roles, I worked as an Equity Research Extern, where I analyzed Amneal Pharmaceuticals' financials, developed a comprehensive valuation model, and delivered a buy recommendation with a detailed price target. Currently pursuing a Bachelor of Business Administration in Finance at Emory University's Goizueta Business School with a secondary major in Data Science, I leverage skills in financial modeling, research, and business communication. I have experience studying and using SQL, Python, R, Excel, and Tableau to solve real-life business problems and analyze data sets. I have lived in Korea, China, Germany, Turkey, and the US. Constantly adapting to new systems taught me to stay comfortable with ambiguity, pick up context fast, and see problems from more than one vantage point, which is a large part of why I’m drawn to global finance and infrastructure, where the interesting work happens at the intersection of markets, policy, and borders.

My projects range from backtesting engines and portfolio risk tools to statistical analyses of sports, climate and text data, all built in Python.
